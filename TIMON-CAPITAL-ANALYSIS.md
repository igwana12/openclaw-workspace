```
Waza → TransFi (direct counterparty, suit filed) → Plaude (bad actor, $12-20M frozen at US banks)
```

### Actions Matrix

| Action | Effort | Probability | Expected Value |
|--------|--------|-------------|----------------|

1. **Pause active litigation spend** - Waza cannot afford it, Timon funding legal fees for ~$850k recovery is poor ROI given uncertain TransFi/Plaude solvency.

2. **Paper maximum optionality:**
   - Get **written acknowledgment from Maxwell** of Timon's $1M senior position
   - Document that any future Waza equity raise or exit triggers repayment priority
   - Consider **convertible note or warrant coverage** for the $850k outstanding
   - Ensure any TransFi settlement flows to senior creditors (Timon) before equity

3. **Monitoring brief:**
   - Track Plaude's US bank cases (FOIA requests, PACER for litigation)
   - If Plaude assets unfreeze, TransFi may settle → Waza may recover → Timon may recover
   - Low-cost monitoring, no active spend

4. **On extrajudicial options:**
   - The PI work confirming Plaude CEO location was reasonable due diligence
   - Further pressure should be legal/reputational, not physical
   - Consider: Does Morrison Foerster have contacts who could apply informal pressure on Plaude? Trade press exposure?

### Key Papering for Optionality
